NFL Fourth-Down Stops Calculator

Derives defensive fourth-down stops from NFLverse play-by-play data for every game in a season. A fourth-down stop is a qualifying opponent fourth-down attempt that does not convert.

NFLverse PBP Derived Stat

Default source: NFLverse season play-by-play release. The browser must be permitted to retrieve the selected URL.
4DS = qualifying opponent 4th-down attempts − qualifying opponent 4th-down conversions

Load a season of NFLverse play-by-play data to calculate fourth-down stops by game and week.

Qualifying plays are fourth-down plays marked as converted or failed in NFLverse data. Punts, field-goal attempts, extra-point attempts, nullified/no-play records, interceptions, and lost fumbles are excluded before deriving attempts, conversions, and stops. Game status is inferred from available NFLverse schedule/play-by-play fields and should be independently verified for league scoring decisions.
